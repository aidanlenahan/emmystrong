We ensure that everyone in need of a bone marrow transplant can find a matching donor.

About EmmyStrong

Founded when Emersyn Freund was diagnosed with leukemia, EmmyStrong has hosted dozens of bone marrow swabbing drives across the country, adding thousands of people to the national registry. Our Executive Board consists of high school students from Monmouth County working tirelessly to support patients in need.
