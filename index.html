Our Story

EmmyStrong was founded when Emersyn Freund was diagnosed with leukemia. Emmy needed a bone marrow transplant but had no donor match in the national bone marrow registry. Friends and the other founding members got to work and hosted dozens of bone marrow swabbing drives in hopes of adding a perfect match for Emmy to the registry. In the process, EmmyStrong was formed.

Through swabbing events across the country, EmmyStrong has added thousands of people to the registry, and our efforts have helped multiple people receive life-saving bone marrow transplants from donors swabbed at EmmyStrong events.

100-Day Milestone Chart

Why Day 100?

Day 100 is the day bone marrow transplant patients circle boldly on their calendars as the turning point in their recovery. By then, the greatest risk for critical side effects has passed, and the new stem cells have engrafted and begun making new blood cells.

EmmyStrong created 100-Day Milestone Charts and Stickers to help patients track their progress toward that milestone. We supply them to hospitals and, through our partnership with the National Marrow Donor Program (NMDP), directly to patients. To request a chart, email EmstrongNJ@gmail.com.

You can help save a life by joining the bone marrow registry through NMDP or our DKMS virtual drive. See our resources page for more.
