Our Story

EmmyStrong was formed when Emersyn Freund was diagnosed with leukemia and needed a bone marrow transplant but had no match in the national registry. Friends and volunteers organized swabbing drives across the country; out of those efforts EmmyStrong was created. To date, EmmyStrong has added thousands of potential donors to the National Bone Marrow Registry and supported multiple patients who received life-saving transplants.
