EmmyStrong Reaches 5,000 Registered Donors

Through dedicated community drives and awareness events, EmmyStrong has surpassed 5,000 registrants on the National Bone Marrow Registry — a huge milestone in our mission to connect patients with donors.

100-Day Milestone Charts Expand Nationwide

Our 100-Day Milestone Charts are now used in hospitals across the U.S., helping bone marrow transplant patients track their progress and stay motivated through recovery.

Partnership with National Marrow Donor Program

EmmyStrong is proud to partner with the NMDP to further expand the registry and provide resources for patients undergoing transplants.
