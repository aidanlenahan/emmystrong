About EmmyStrong

Founded when Emersyn Freund was diagnosed with leukemia, EmmyStrong has worked tirelessly to help patients find life-saving bone marrow matches.

Our Story

When Emersyn Freund needed a bone marrow transplant but had no donor match in the national registry, friends and founding members created EmmyStrong. Hosting dozens of bone marrow swabbing events nationwide, EmmyStrong added thousands of potential donors to the registry and formed a committed nonprofit organization.

Our Mission

Our mission is to ensure that everyone in need of a bone marrow transplant can find a matching donor.

Programs & Support

  • Continuing to host bone marrow swabbing events to increase the registry
  • Providing 100-Day Milestone Charts and Stickers for patients
  • Engaging in peer-to-peer mentorship with transplant patients
  • Holiday support for patients in oncology and transplant units
  • Partnerships with National Marrow Donor Program
  • Organizing fundraisers like “Kick Cancer Overboard”
