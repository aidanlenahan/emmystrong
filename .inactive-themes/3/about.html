About EmmyStrong Foundation

Our Story

Image Placeholder

EmmyStrong was founded when Emersyn Freund was diagnosed with leukemia and required a bone marrow transplant—but had no donor match in the national registry. Friends and local supporters mobilized, hosting dozens of swabbing drives to help find a match for Emmy. In that process, the EmmyStrong Foundation was born.

Since its creation, EmmyStrong has added over 5,000 potential donors to the National Bone Marrow Registry, directly helping multiple individuals receive life-saving transplants.

Mission Statement

Our mission is to ensure that everyone in need of a bone marrow transplant can find a matching donor.
