EmmyStrong Foundation

Our mission is to ensure that everyone in need of a bone marrow transplant can find a matching donor.

Our Story

Founded when Emersyn Freund needed a bone marrow transplant, EmmyStrong has hosted dozens of swabbing events, adding thousands of donors to the national registry.

Impact

  • Added over 5,000 bone marrow donors to the National Registry.
  • Provided 100-Day Milestone Charts for patients.
  • Supported multiple patients in receiving life-saving transplants.
