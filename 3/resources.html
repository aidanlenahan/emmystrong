Resources & Support

For Patients & Families

  • 100-Day Milestone Chart & Sticker Program — Track your recovery progress with our free kits provided to hospitals and patients.
  • Peer-to-Peer Sessions — Connect with Emmy and other adolescents who have undergone bone marrow transplants.
  • Holiday Gift Drives — Annual holiday events providing gifts to inpatient oncology and transplant units.

For Volunteers

Join our volunteer team and help organize swabbing events, pack patient care kits, and spread awareness in your community.
