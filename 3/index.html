Our Mission

Our mission is to ensure that everyone in need of a bone marrow transplant can find a matching donor. Since our founding, EmmyStrong has added over 5,000 donors to the National Bone Marrow Registry and supported countless families through the transplant process.

Bone Marrow Swabbing Drives

We host events across the nation to grow the registry and give hope to patients like Emmy.

Patient Support

From milestone charts to peer connections, we stand beside transplant patients every step of the way.
