About EmmyStrong

Mission Statement

Our mission is to ensure that everyone in need of a bone marrow transplant can find a matching donor.

Executive Board

  • Emersyn Freund - President
  • Jolie Trezza - Vice President
  • Emma Jaroschak - Secretary
  • Tyler Gibert - Director of Creative Services
  • Sam Healy - Corresponding Secretary

History

EmmyStrong was founded after Emersyn Freund’s leukemia diagnosis and lack of a donor match. Friends organized swabbing events nationwide, creating EmmyStrong and adding thousands of potential donors to the registry.
