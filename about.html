About EmmyStrong

Founded to find a bone marrow match for Emersyn Freund, EmmyStrong now works to grow the national registry and support bone marrow transplant patients.

Our Mission

“Our mission is to ensure that everyone in need of a bone marrow transplant can find a matching donor.”

Our History

EmmyStrong was founded when Emersyn Freund was diagnosed with leukemia. Emmy needed a bone marrow transplant but had no donor match in the national bone marrow registry. Friends and the other founding members got to work and hosted dozens of bone marrow swabbing drives in hopes of adding a perfect match for Emmy to the registry. In the process, EmmyStrong was formed.

Through swabbing events across the country, EmmyStrong has added over 5,000 bone marrow donors to the national registry. Our efforts have helped multiple people receive life-saving bone marrow transplants from donors swabbed at EmmyStrong events.

How We Help

EmmyStrong supports patients in need of a bone marrow transplant in many ways:

  • Hosting bone marrow swabbing events to keep adding potential donors to the national registry.
  • Hosting holiday present drives for patients spending the holidays in inpatient oncology and bone marrow transplant units.
  • Creating and distributing 100-Day Milestone Charts and Stickers so bone marrow patients can track their progress toward the 100-day post-transplant milestone.
  • Supplying 100-Day Milestone Charts and Stickers to hospitals, including Children’s Hospital of Philadelphia, Hackensack University Medical Center, Johns Hopkins Hospital, and Massachusetts General Hospital.
  • Partnering with the National Marrow Donor Program (NMDP), the national bone marrow registry, to provide charts and stickers directly to patients and to other bone marrow units.
  • Partnering with NMDP on projects to further grow the national bone marrow registry.
  • Offering peer-to-peer sessions between Emmy and adolescents undergoing bone marrow transplants.
  • Organizing fundraisers to cover the costs of the 100-Day Milestone Charts and Stickers.
  • Raising enough funds through the Kick Cancer Overboard initiative to send a family affected by cancer on a cruise.
